## Current scope decision

Until participants are available and ethics approval and hardware/timing details are confirmed, any future real-time work is a **self-test/demo only**. Do not recruit, collect participant data, or make a live-study claim. The later own-data phase remains gated on these prerequisites.

## Before a real-time pilot

- Record the hardware and electrode montage, reference, sample rate, and input range.
- Verify marker timing and LSL availability, including a loopback or photodiode timing check.
- Complete the applicable ethics approval, consent, privacy, data-retention, and photosensitivity-screening process.
- Establish participant access and the pilot protocol before scheduling any data collection.
